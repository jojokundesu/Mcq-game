## ✨ What's inside

**🔎 Instant search** — type 2–3 letters of any *salt* ("amox", "metform") OR *brand* ("zerodol", "brufen") → live suggestion dropdown + full info card (ingredients, formulation, strength, dose, clinical note, ₹ rate, category, plus "same-salt brands" chips).

**🎯 MCQ game, 2 modes**
- 💊 **Brand → Salts**: shown a brand, pick its composition
- 🧂 **Salts → Brand**: shown a composition, pick the only matching brand — distractor uniqueness **machine-verified 4,000/4,000** (exactly ONE correct option, always)
- Scoring with 🔥 streaks, ⏱ timer bonus, ✂ 50:50 lifelines, personal bests, 📣 share-score, confetti, sounds & haptics

**🪟 Never-stuck UI** — every popup minimises into a floating bottom pill; long-press pill to restore.

**📦 Data integrity** — 1,789 source rows processed; **93 compositions web-verified** (1mg/PharmEasy/Apollo/manufacturer sites); devices/sundries reclassified as non-drug; **91 unverifiable items skipped** with full honesty (list inside the app: More → About, and in `SKIPPED_MEDICINES.md`).
